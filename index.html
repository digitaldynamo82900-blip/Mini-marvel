Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum age for admission? +

We accept children from 3 months onwards in our Infant Care program. We have programs designed for every developmental stage from 3 months to 5 years.

What are your working hours? +

Our school operates from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday. We also offer extended care till 5:00 PM for an additional fee. Saturday activities are available on request.

Do you provide transportation? +

Yes, we provide safe and secure transportation services. Transportation fee is ₹500/month, and all vehicles are equipped with trackers and first-aid kits.

What meals are provided? +

We provide nutritious home-style meals including lunch and snacks. Meals are prepared fresh daily, and we accommodate dietary preferences and allergies. Special care is taken for younger children.

How do you ensure child safety? +

Safety is our top priority. We have CCTV surveillance in all areas, trained staff in first-aid and CPR, secure entry/exit protocols, and regular safety drills. Parents receive daily updates via our app.

What is your sick child policy? +

If a child is sick (fever, vomiting, diarrhea), they should not attend school. We require a doctor's clearance before readmission. In case of emergencies, we contact parents immediately.

Do you follow any specific curriculum? +

We follow a play-based, child-centered approach that blends with the Montessori philosophy and NCERT guidelines. We focus on developmental milestones rather than rote learning.

How often do you communicate with parents? +

We maintain daily communication through our school app, monthly parent meetings, and quarterly progress reports. Teachers are available for individual consultations as needed.

School Policies

📋 Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is important for your child's development. We maintain 80% attendance as the minimum requirement. Absences should be communicated in advance when possible.

🚫 Pick-up & Drop-off Policy

Only authorized guardians listed during admission are allowed to pick up children. We verify ID before release. Late pick-ups after 4:00 PM incur a charge of ₹50 per 15 minutes.

💊 Medication Policy

Any medication must be provided in original containers with proper labeling. We require written authorization from parents. Our staff will administer medication as prescribed.

🍽️ Allergy & Dietary Policy

We maintain detailed records of all allergies. Meals are customized accordingly. Parents must inform us of any new allergies immediately.

📸 Photography & Privacy Policy

We take photos for learning documentation and developmental records. We respect privacy and do not share images without explicit parental consent.

💰 Fee Payment Policy

Monthly fees are due by the 5th of each month. We offer flexible payment options. Late fees of ₹100/day apply after the 10th. Annual maintenance charge is ₹1,000.
